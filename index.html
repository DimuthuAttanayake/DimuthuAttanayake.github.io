Hello! I'm Dimuthu Attanayake, a data journalist reporting on business, AI, climate, and global geopolitics.

Over the last decade, I've worked as the Sri Lanka contributor to the South China Morning Post, and an enterprise and investigative reporter, with cross-border investigations for The Guardian, Deutsche Welle, and Nikkei Asia.

My reporting for the Thomson Reuters Foundation on climate and gender-based violence won the 2024 Developing Asia Journalism Award from the Asian Development Bank Institute (ADBI), and the full series on climate and its gendered impacts on farmers, reported for Foreign Policy, the Thomson Reuters Foundation, and the South China Morning Post, received an honourable mention at the 2026 Journalism Excellence Awards by the Asian American Journalists Association (AAJA). I was the South Asia representative of the UN Reham Al-Farra Memorial Journalism Fellowship covering the UN General Assembly and Security Council.
