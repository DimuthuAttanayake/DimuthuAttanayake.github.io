Hello! I'm Dimuthu Attanayake, a data journalist reporting on climate, environment, and global geopolitics.

Over the last decade, I've worked across reporting, research, and business advisory — with cross-border investigations for The Guardian, South China Morning Post, Deutsche Welle, Nikkei Asia, Foreign Policy, and the BBC.

I'm currently a graduate student in the Data Journalism program at Columbia University. My Pulitzer Center reporting on climate and gender-based violence won the 2024 Developing Asia Journalism Award, and I served as South Asia representative of the UN Reham Al-Farra Memorial Journalism Fellowship covering the UN General Assembly.
